Led the development of Prophecy at Amazon ReCommerce, a warehouse optimization system that transformed how Amazon handles returned and damaged products.

## The Challenge

Amazon's reverse logistics operation processes massive volumes of returned and damaged inventory daily. The existing system relied on hard-coded business rules that couldn't adapt to changing conditions or optimize for economic recovery.

## The Solution

Prophecy replaces rigid business rules with algorithmic routing that considers multiple factors to make optimal decisions about each item's disposition—whether to restock, refurbish, liquidate, or recycle.

## Impact

- **Scale:** Powers 1+ billion decisions annually
- **Flexibility:** Adapts to changing business conditions dynamically
- **Recovery:** Improved economic recovery of no-longer-new inventory

## Technical Details

The system integrates with warehouse management systems to provide real-time routing decisions, balancing throughput requirements with optimization goals.
